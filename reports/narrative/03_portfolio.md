We start from Markowitz and CAPM, and report the standard KPIs, but textbook mean-variance pushes the portfolio into corner positions. We therefore build the frontier by minimising variance (Ledoit-Wolf, 756 days) at each equity share under the caps, and use forward-looking returns only to score the points.

Expected returns are built from today's yields: AGG is the 10-year yield plus 0.5%; equities are the yield plus beta times a 4.5% premium; gold earns breakeven inflation; fees, withholding tax and the volatility drag are deducted.

The rule picks the lowest-risk point that meets the objective inside every limit. At the decision date that is 35% equity: SPY 15.6%, EFA 9.4%, AGG 65.0%, XLE 10.0%. At 30% the return falls short; at 40% the crisis loss breaks the budget. AGG and XLE are at their caps at this point, which we disclose.

Walk-forward since 2008 the strategy earned 5.8% a year with a worst fall of 21.1%. The 60/40 benchmark earned 8.1% with a worst fall of 33.5%, and it breaks the mandate's limits.
