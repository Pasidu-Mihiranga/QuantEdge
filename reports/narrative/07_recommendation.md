Position sizing uses each fund's contribution to Expected Shortfall. No fund carries more than 40% of the tail risk (the largest is SPY at 33%), so no resizing was needed.

The locked rule gives **ACCEPT at 35% equity**. We fixed in advance that the verdict would turn on real yields: on end-2022 yields the same rule gave REJECT, and we recorded that a 10-year TIPS yield of about 2.58% would flip it. On the decision date it was 2.66%.

The margin is thin: ACCEPT is lost if the real yield falls 0.15 points or if the equity premium is below 4.15%. Across 18 assumption combinations the verdict is ACCEPT in 7, ADJUST in 4 and REJECT in 7. The risk limits hold in all of them.

Governance: rebalance quarterly; the committee meets if the risk forecast stays above the limit for 5 days, since an automatic selling rule made losses worse when tested; review spending, limits and assumptions every year; and take tax advice before investing.
