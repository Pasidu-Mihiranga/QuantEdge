We replay the 2008 crisis, the March 2020 crash and the 2022 rate shock with today's weights held at target, the cautious convention, and add a scenario of our own design.

- **GFC 2008:** 23.9%, the closest to the budget. Bonds helped.
- **COVID 2020:** 18.8%, deepened by the energy holding as oil collapsed.
- **Rate shock 2022:** 13.8%. Stocks and bonds fell together.
- **Two recent episodes fixed before we looked:** the 2025 tariff shock (5.7%) and the 2026 Hormuz shock (2.9%), where energy gained.

**Hormuz stagflation 2027** is our scenario: 10-year yield +250 bp, oil +40%, VIX +25, dollar +8%, credit +150 bp over three quarters, plus one panic day on AGG. It costs 21.0%. It is the most severe candidate still within history, and the factor mapping reproduced 2022 (17.8% predicted against 18.8% actual). A version beyond the record would cost about 25.3%.

Reverse stress shows the likeliest route to a 25% loss is a broad equity crash with bonds not helping.
