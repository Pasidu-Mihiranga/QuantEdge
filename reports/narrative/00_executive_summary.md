The Serendib University Endowment Fund asks for capital preservation, income and modest real growth, and it values avoiding large losses above maximising gains. We recommend that the committee **accepts the mandate as proposed**: invest 35% in equities and 65% in bonds, with the objective of US inflation + 3.5% a year. The result comes from a rule we fixed before looking at the final test data, and it is a close call that we state openly.

- **Daily risk:** about USD 208,000 on the worst 1 day in 100 through the cycle, against a limit of about USD 226,000.

The margin is thin. The verdict rests on an assumed 4.5% equity premium and on AGG's interest being free of US withholding tax. It holds in 7 of 18 assumption combinations, and the chance of meeting the objective over ten years is about even. We ask the committee to treat the objective as a target, not a promise, and to take tax advice before investing.
