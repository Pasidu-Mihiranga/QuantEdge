- **Daily risk:** an average loss of about USD 208,000 on the worst 1 day in 100 through the cycle, against a limit of about USD 226,000.
- **Model:** GJR conditional EVT passed a one-shot test on 2023–2026 (9 breaches against 9.6 expected).

The margin is narrow. The decision rests on an assumed 4.5% equity premium and on AGG's interest being free of US withholding tax. It holds in 7 of 18 assumption combinations, and the 10-year chance of meeting the objective is about even. We state this openly and ask the committee to treat the objective as a target and to take tax advice before investing.
