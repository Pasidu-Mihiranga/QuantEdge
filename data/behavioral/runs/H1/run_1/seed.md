## Market participants
- **institutional**: Pension funds and endowments: long horizon, benchmark-aware, rebalance mechanically, sell risk only when limits bind.
- **retail**: Individual investors: trend-following, sensitive to headlines and recent losses, prone to panic selling and fund outflows.
- **bond**: Fixed-income managers: duration-focused, react to policy-rate guidance and inflation prints, flee to short maturities in rate shocks.
- **speculative**: Hedge funds and leveraged traders: momentum and macro bets, forced to de-lever when volatility and margins rise.
- **conservative**: Capital-preservation investors: seek gold, high-quality bonds and cash in stress; slow to re-enter risk.
